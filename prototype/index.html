📚 100-Page Agricultural Pathology Compendium

Standardized disease profiles and macroscopic lesion taxonomy for agronomists and students.

Fungal · Alternaria

Tomato Early Blight

Produces brown circular necrotic spots with concentric ridges ('bullseye' or target-board pattern). Lower foliage is infected first.

Rx: Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or NSKE 5%
Oomycete · Phytophthora

Potato / Tomato Late Blight

Causes water-soaked dark purplish lesions that rapidly enlarge under cool, damp conditions. White fungal mycelium appears on undersides.

Rx: Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (Ridomil) @ 2.0 g/L
Bacterial · Xanthomonas

Rice Bacterial Leaf Blight

Water-soaked to yellowish-white wavy stripes extending downward from leaf margins. Milky bacterial ooze forms early morning.

Rx: Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L + Copper Oxychloride
Fungal · Puccinia

Corn Common Rust

Cinnamon-brown to golden powdery pustules (uredinia) that break through the epidermis on both upper and lower leaf surfaces.

Rx: Azoxystrobin + Difenoconazole @ 1.0 mL/L
Physiological Control

Healthy Leaf Tissue

Uniform vibrant green photosynthetic lamina, intact cellular venation, zero chlorosis or necrotic spotting.

Rx: Balanced N-P-K mineral nutrition
